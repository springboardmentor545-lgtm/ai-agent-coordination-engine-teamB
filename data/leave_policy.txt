COMPANY LEAVE POLICY (Reference Document)

1. Minimum Notice Period: All leave requests must be submitted at least 3 days before the requested start date, except in genuine emergencies (subject to manager discretion).

2. Leave Balance: Employees may not request more leave days than their current available balance.

3. Team Coverage: If more than 30% of an employee's department is on leave during the same period, the request will be escalated to the manager for review rather than automatically approved, to ensure adequate team coverage.

4. All decisions are logged and can be reviewed by HR upon request.
